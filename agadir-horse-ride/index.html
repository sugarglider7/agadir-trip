The experience

What this ride is really like

Most Agadir horse rides stick to a stretch of beach. This one leaves from a ranch by the Souss river mouth and threads a route you won't find on foot: river banks, shaded eucalyptus forest, and a pass by the walls of the royal palace grounds.

An experienced guide rides with you the whole way and sets the pace to the group. Back at the ranch, you're poured Moroccan mint tea with snacks before the transfer returns you to your hotel.

Highlights

  • Ride the Souss river banks and eucalyptus forest — not just a beach loop
  • Pass the royal palace grounds on the southern edge of Agadir

Who it's for

Riders of all levels — the guide adapts the route's pace to the group — and travellers who want a greener, quieter side of Agadir than the beachfront. Children under 10 are welcome too.

Questions about this tour

Do I need riding experience?

No — an experienced guide accompanies every ride and sets the pace to the group. Let us know your level when you book so the right horse is prepared.

Can children join?

Yes — children under 10 are welcome. Tell us ages in your booking message so the ranch can plan and confirm the child rate.

Where exactly do you ride?

From the ranch near the Souss river mouth: around the ranch, along the river, through the eucalyptus forest and past the royal palace grounds.
