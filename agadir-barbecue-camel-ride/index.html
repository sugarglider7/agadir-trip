The experience

What this ride is really like

This is the ride the operator is known for: a slow camel caravan timed so you cross the Souss river banks, the small dunes and the eucalyptus forest just as the light turns gold. It ends the way every good Moroccan evening should — around a charcoal barbecue back at the ranch.

Dinner is simple and generous: Moroccan salad and chicken grilled over coals, with a vegetarian option if you ask for it when you book. While you ride, the guides take photos of you on their own initiative — hands free, no selfie acrobatics on camelback required.

Highlights

  • Camel caravan at sunset through the Souss river area, dunes and eucalyptus forest
  • Moroccan barbecue at the ranch: salad + chicken grilled over coals (vegetarian option available)
  • Guides photograph you during the ride — you just enjoy it
  • Pickup and return transfer from Agadir hotels, Taghazout or the Cruise Port

How the evening unfolds

  1. Late afternoon

    Hotel pickup

    Your driver collects you from your hotel in Agadir, Taghazout or the Cruise Port. The exact time depends on the season's sunset — it's confirmed when you book.

  2. At the ranch

    Meet your camel

    A short introduction at the ranch near the Souss river, then the caravan sets off.

  3. Golden hour

    Ride into the sunset

    The caravan winds through the river banks, dunes and eucalyptus forest as the light fades, while the guides take photos of the group.

  4. After dark

    Barbecue at the ranch

    Moroccan salad and chicken barbecue cooked over charcoal — vegetarian option if requested in advance.

  5. Evening

    Return transfer

    You're driven back to your hotel.

Who it's for

Couples, families and first-time riders. The pace is walking pace, the camels are led by the guides, and the published child price (under 10: €20) makes it an easy family evening.

Questions about this tour

Is there a vegetarian option at the barbecue?

Yes — the operator offers a vegetarian alternative to the chicken barbecue. Mention it in your WhatsApp message when you book.

What time does the ride start?

It's timed around sunset, so pickup time changes with the season. The exact pickup time is confirmed with you when you book.

Can children ride?

Yes — this tour has a published child price of €20 for children under 10, and camels are led at walking pace by the guides.
