The experience

What this tour is really like

The buggy runs the same wild route as the quad tour — Berber village country, pre-Saharan scrub, then the Tifnit dunes with the Atlantic glittering behind them — but you share the ride. Driver plus passenger, roll cage, bucket seats.

That makes it the pick for couples and for anyone who wants the dunes without handlebars. A ten-minute orientation covers the controls, and helmet and goggles are included. Mixed quad-and-buggy groups ride together from the same base.

Highlights

  • Two seats — share the ride instead of following on separate machines
  • Tifnit beach dunes and Atlantic views south of Agadir
  • 10-minute orientation, helmet and goggles included
  • Same base as the quads — mixed groups welcome

How the tour unfolds

  1. Start

    Hotel pickup

    Pickup from Agadir hotels, Taghazout or the Cruise Port, then the transfer south to the base.

  2. At the base

    Orientation

    Ten minutes on controls and the route, helmets and goggles fitted.

  3. ≈ 2 hours

    Dunes & Atlantic

    Guided drive through Berber rural country and pre-Saharan terrain to the Tifnit dunes, with the ocean alongside.

  4. End

    Return transfer

    Back to base and home to your hotel.

Who it's for

Couples and pairs who'd rather share one machine, and groups mixing quads and buggies. Each buggy seats a driver plus a passenger — ask when you book whether the rate covers the buggy or each seat.
